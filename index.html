How it works

Intelligence that works behind the scenes

STEP 1

Upload photos

Drop photos from your camera roll, any folder, or directly from your camera. Frame accepts all formats and handles the rest.

📸
STEP 2

AI detection

Frame analyzes faces, timestamps, and locations to understand what happened. It recognizes events automatically without manual tagging.

🔍
STEP 3

Creates event albums

Photos are grouped into beautiful, shareable albums. Each album captures a complete moment — no manual sorting needed.

📚
STEP 4

Sends secure links

Frame generates a private link and notifies everyone in the photos. They can view, download, and contribute their own pictures.

The research journey

From initial observations to final product — GR1 through GR10

GR1

Needfinding

Conduct user interviews and synthesize findings to identify meaningful user needs and opportunity areas.

SCOPE

User interviews, needfinding synthesis, empathy maps, and key insights.

DELIVERABLE

Slide deck PDF on Canvas plus all additional items listed in GR1.

GR2

POVs, HMWs & Experience Prototypes

Extend research and translate findings into problem framing and early prototype directions.

SCOPE

Add 2+ interviews, define 2-3 POVs, generate 10+ HMWs per POV, and build experience prototypes.

DELIVERABLE

Slide deck PDF on Canvas plus all additional items listed in GR2.

GR3

Project Website

Build and maintain a project website for an external audience that clearly presents the project and progress.

SCOPE

Link key deliverables and keep the site polished with final artifact links.

DELIVERABLE

Project website hosted on github.io.

GR4

Concept Video

Create a concept video that communicates the core problem, proposed solution, and why it matters.

SCOPE

Cover stakeholders, market research, and ethical considerations.

DELIVERABLE

Slides to Canvas and concept video to Google Drive and YouTube.

GR5

Sketching, Low-Fi & User Testing

Explore many ideas through sketching, then diverge and converge toward a smaller set of promising directions.

SCOPE

Build low-fi flows/screens, run pilot usability tests, and use GR5 peer feedback plus TA rubric guidance.

DELIVERABLE

Slide deck PDF on Canvas plus all additional items listed in GR5.

GR6

Medium-Fi Figma Prototype

Build an interactive medium-fi Figma prototype based on GR5 pilot testing and TA feedback.

SCOPE

This prototype becomes the basis for the GR7 heuristic evaluation.

DELIVERABLE

Medium-fi interactive prototype on Figma.
GR7

Heuristic Evaluation (Individual)

Conduct a heuristic evaluation of other teams' GR6a medium-fi prototypes.

SCOPE

Identify usability issues, rate severity, and provide clear recommendations.

DELIVERABLE

Evaluation spreadsheet as listed in GR7.

GR8

Hi-Fi Figma Prototype

Create a high-fi Figma prototype that incorporates improvements from GR7 heuristic evaluation feedback.

SCOPE

Refine visual design, interaction detail, and overall usability in the updated prototype.

DELIVERABLE

Updated high-fi Figma prototype.

GR9

Poster, Pitch & Video

Prepare final presentation materials centered on the GR8 hi-fi prototype.

SCOPE

Final presentation signup, 5-minute team pitch plus 1.5-minute Q&A, and aligned project assets.

DELIVERABLE

One pitch slide, one poster slide, and a demo video uploaded to the team Drive folder.

GR10

Final Report

Write the final project narrative that covers work across all GRs.

SCOPE

Document the full process, key decisions, iterations, and final outcomes in one cohesive report.

DELIVERABLE

Final report submitted as a Google Doc.
